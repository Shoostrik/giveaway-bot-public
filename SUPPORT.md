# Support

## English

Giveaway Bot is not publicly launched yet. Active support channels, official links and response expectations will be published after they are verified.

For now:

- read the [README](README.md) and [FAQ](FAQ.md);
- follow verified progress in [PROJECT_UPDATES.md](PROJECT_UPDATES.md);

For security concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
